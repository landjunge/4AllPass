## netzwerkpunkt.de Logo (new request)

**Concept direction:**
"Netzwerk-Punkt" = the central junction / glowing node that connects all projects.

Ideas:
1. A luminous central point/node with subtle radiating network lines or constellation connections.
2. Minimal geometric node that also works as favicon.
3. Clean wordmark "netzwerkpunkt" with an integrated glowing dot/point.
4. Slightly abstract: a point that feels like both a network hub and an observation point (portfolio overview).

Style: modern, clean, slightly experimental but professional. Should feel like the parent brand above 4AllPass, Tollgate and Gnom-Hub-V1.

Color suggestion: Deep dark base + soft teal / indigo or warm gold accents to stay related but distinct from the pure Golden Magpie of 4AllPass.
